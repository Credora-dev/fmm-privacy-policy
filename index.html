Privacy Policy for Friends of Mother Mary (FMM)

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Friends of Mother Mary (FMM) is a digital spiritual library that allows users to discover and read Catholic and spiritual books. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide when using the app.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, FMM may collect the following information:

  • Email address: collected when you create an account or sign in.
  • Password: used for account authentication. Passwords are handled securely by our authentication service and are not stored by us in plain text.
  • Profile photo: optional. You may choose to upload a profile photo to your account.
  • Profile information: information you choose to provide when creating or managing your account.

You may be able to browse and use parts of the FMM application without creating or signing into an account.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through FMM may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide access to features of the application.
  • Allow you to manage your profile.
  • Provide and improve the digital library experience.
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of the application.

3. Data Storage and Security

FMM uses third-party services, including Firebase, to provide certain application functions such as authentication and data storage. We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may use trusted service providers that process information on our behalf when necessary to operate the application and provide its services.

5. Profile Photos

Uploading a profile photo is optional. If you choose to upload one, it may be stored and displayed as part of your FMM user profile.

6. Children's Privacy

FMM is not specifically designed as a children's application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

7. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services such as Firebase to provide authentication, database, storage, hosting, analytics, security, or other technical services.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
